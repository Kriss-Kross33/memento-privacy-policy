Memento

Privacy Policy

Memento is designed to help you organize and keep track of the things that matter to you, while keeping your personal information primarily on your device.

Effective date: September 20, 2026

01 — Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Memento handles information when you use the Memento mobile application.

Memento is designed as a local-first application. Your personal receipt information, images, OCR results, documents, and related organizational data are stored locally on your device rather than being uploaded to servers operated by Memento.

Your receipt content stays on your device. Memento does not upload your receipts, receipt photos, OCR results, or documents to Memento servers.

02 — Information Stored on Your Device

Memento stores information locally on your device to provide its core functionality.

This may include:

  • Receipt information and details you enter or create
  • Receipt photographs and images
  • OCR results extracted from receipt images
  • Documents and files you add to the app
  • Categories and organizational information
  • App preferences and settings
  • Other information created as part of using Memento

This information is stored locally within the application's storage on your device. Memento does not operate a server that receives and stores your receipt content.

03 — Receipt Scanning & OCR

Memento can use your device's camera or existing images to help capture receipt information.

Where OCR is used, text recognition is performed on the device using the relevant platform or on-device recognition technology. The resulting text is used to help organize and populate your receipt information within Memento.

Receipt images and OCR results are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to Memento servers for processing.

04 — Purchases & Subscriptions

Memento may offer optional paid features or subscriptions.

Purchases are processed through Google Play on Android. Memento uses RevenueCat to manage subscription information and entitlement status so that the application can determine which purchased features are available to you.

Purchase-related information may therefore be processed by Google Play and RevenueCat in connection with your subscription and purchases.

Memento does not receive or store your full payment card, bank-account, or other payment credentials used to complete a purchase through Google Play.

Google Play and RevenueCat process information according to their respective privacy practices and policies.

05 — Accounts

Memento does not currently require you to create an account to use its core functionality.

Your locally stored Memento data is associated with the application on your device rather than with a Memento account maintained on our servers.

Subscription and purchase services may use identifiers required by the relevant payment and subscription infrastructure. These identifiers are separate from a Memento user account.

06 — Notifications

Memento may schedule local notifications and reminders on your device.

These notifications are used to provide app-related reminders and functionality. Memento does not require a Memento server to deliver these local notifications.

You can control notification permissions through your device's operating-system settings.

07 — Information We Do Not Store on Memento Servers

Memento's current architecture does not use a Memento-operated cloud database for your personal receipt content.

In particular, Memento does not upload the following to Memento servers:

  • Receipt content
  • Receipt photographs
  • OCR results
  • Documents you store in the application
  • Your locally created categories and organization data

This may change if future versions introduce optional cloud synchronization or other online services. If that happens, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are used to process your information.

08 — Third-Party Services

Memento relies on certain third-party services to provide specific functionality.

Service Purpose
Google Play App distribution and processing purchases and subscriptions on Android.
RevenueCat Subscription management, purchase information, and entitlement status.
On-device OCR technology Extracting text from receipt images on the device.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

09 — Data Security

Memento is designed to minimize the amount of personal content that leaves your device. Receipt information, images, OCR results, and documents are stored locally rather than being uploaded to Memento servers.

Communications with third-party services used by Memento for purchases and subscriptions are transmitted using the security mechanisms provided by those services.

You are also responsible for maintaining the security of your device, including its screen lock, operating-system security, and access to the device's stored data.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10 — Data Retention & Deletion

Memento's locally stored receipt information, images, OCR results, documents, and related data remain on your device until you delete them through the application or remove the application's stored data.

Uninstalling Memento may remove locally stored application data, depending on your device and operating-system behavior.

Purchase and subscription information processed by Google Play or RevenueCat is handled according to the retention practices and requirements applicable to those services.

11 — Children's Privacy

Memento is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly use Memento to collect personal information from children through a Memento-operated online service.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through an online service operated by Memento, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

12 — Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Memento's functionality, services, or data practices change.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published at this location with a revised effective date.
