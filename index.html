Information we collect

The information Memento collects depends on how you use the app and the features you choose to use.

Information you provide

You may provide information directly to Memento when creating an account, creating or editing content, configuring preferences, contacting us, or otherwise using features of the app.

This may include information such as your name, email address, profile information, and the content you choose to save in Memento.

Information collected automatically

When you use Memento, certain technical information may be collected automatically. This can include information about your device, operating system, application version, and basic diagnostic information.

We use this information where necessary to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

02

How we use information

Information collected through Memento is used for purposes such as:

  • Providing and operating the Memento app.
  • Saving and displaying the content you create.
  • Maintaining your account and preferences.
  • Providing features you choose to use.
  • Responding to support requests.
  • Detecting and addressing technical problems.
  • Protecting the security and integrity of the service.
  • Improving the reliability and usability of Memento.

We only use information for purposes that are reasonably connected to operating and improving the service, providing features you request, or meeting legal and security requirements.

03

Storage & security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Memento against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Depending on the feature and version of Memento you are using, information may be stored locally on your device or on infrastructure used to provide the service.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

How long information is kept

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain legitimate business and security records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or otherwise fulfill the purposes described in this policy.

04

Sharing information

Memento does not sell your personal information.

We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate the app, such as infrastructure, hosting, analytics, crash reporting, payment, or customer-support providers where those services are used by Memento.

These providers receive only the information reasonably necessary for them to provide their services.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect the rights and safety of users, to investigate abuse or security incidents, or as part of a legitimate business transaction such as a merger or acquisition.

05

Third-party services

Memento may rely on third-party services to provide certain functionality. These services may process limited information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

The specific services used by Memento may change as the app develops. Where a third-party service handles personal information on our behalf, we expect that provider to use appropriate safeguards and only process information for the services they provide.

If Memento introduces a new service that materially changes how personal information is handled, this policy may be updated accordingly.

06

Your choices

Depending on the features available in your version of Memento, you may have choices regarding your information.

Access and correction

You may contact us if you believe information associated with your account is inaccurate and request that it be corrected.

Deletion

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your account, subject to information that we are required or permitted to retain by law.

Notifications

Memento may use notifications for features you have enabled. You can control notification permissions through your device settings.

Device permissions

If Memento requests access to device features such as notifications, photos, files, or other permissions, you can generally manage those permissions through your device settings.

07

Children's privacy

Memento is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to independently provide consent to the processing of their personal information.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

08

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as Memento evolves or as our privacy practices or legal obligations change.

When we make changes, we will update the date shown at the top of this page. If a change is material, we may provide additional notice through the app or another appropriate channel.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how Memento handles information.
